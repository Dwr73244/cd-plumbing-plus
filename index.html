Rochester plumbing questions, answered straight

How much does a plumber cost in Rochester NY?

Most Rochester plumbers charge $85–$175 per hour, with a service-call minimum of $75–$125. C&D Plumbing Plus uses flat-rate pricing instead — we quote the entire job up front so you know the total before we start, with no per-hour surprises. Most common repairs land between $150–$650; water heater replacements typically run $1,400–$2,800 installed.

How long does a water heater last in Rochester NY?

A standard tank water heater lasts 8–12 years in Rochester — slightly shorter than the national average because of our hard water. Tankless units typically last 18–22 years with annual flushing. If yours is over 10 years old and showing rust at the base, popping noises, or rising energy bills, it's time to plan a replacement.

How often do I need backflow testing in Monroe County?

Monroe County requires annual backflow prevention device testing for most commercial properties, irrigation systems, and any home with a fire suppression system or auxiliary water source (well + city). Testing must be done by a NYS-certified backflow tester. C&D is certified, and we file the paperwork with the county for you.

Why do my pipes keep freezing in Rochester winters?

Rochester pipes freeze most often in uninsulated exterior walls, crawlspaces, garages, and basement rim joists where wind chill drops the cavity below 20°F. Older homes with galvanized lines are especially vulnerable. Quick prevention: add pipe insulation, seal air leaks at the rim joist, and keep cabinet doors open under exterior-wall sinks during deep freezes.

Does homeowners insurance cover burst pipes in New York?

Most NY homeowners policies cover sudden, accidental burst pipes — including the water damage they cause — but not the cost of repairing the pipe itself. They generally exclude damage from gradual leaks, or from freezing if your home was left unheated. Call us first; we'll document the failure for your insurance claim.
